Business Sales Performance Analytics

Project Overview:-

This project analyzes business sales data to identify revenue trends, top-selling products, category performance, regional sales performance, and the impact of discounts and sales channels. The goal is to generate meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

Objectives:-

- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Compare sales performance across regions.
- Evaluate sales channels and discount strategies.
- Examine product returns and their impact on sales.

Tools and Technologies:-

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- VS Code
- Git and GitHub

Data Analysis Process:-

1. Loaded and inspected the sales dataset.
2. Checked data types and missing values.
3. Cleaned and prepared the data for analysis.
4. Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA).
5. Analyzed revenue, products, categories, regions, channels, discounts, and returns.
6. Created visualizations to communicate key findings.

Key Findings:-

- March 2023 recorded the highest monthly revenue.
- October recorded the lowest monthly revenue.
- The North region generated the highest revenue, while the South region generated the lowest.
- Retail generated slightly more revenue than Wholesale.
- Transactions with no discount generated the highest revenue among the discount groups analyzed.
- Approximately 24.8% of recorded transactions were marked as returned.

Business Recommendations:-

- Investigate the factors behind the strong performance in March and apply successful strategies during weaker months.
- Analyze the North region's performance to identify practices that could improve sales in the South.
- Continue monitoring Retail and Wholesale performance to optimize channel strategies.
- Evaluate discount effectiveness using profit margins as well as revenue.
- Investigate product returns to identify opportunities to improve product quality and customer satisfaction.

This project demonstrates how Python-based data analysis can reveal sales trends, regional differences, channel performance, discount patterns, and return rates to support better business decisions.
